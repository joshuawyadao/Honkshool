# Honkshool design direction

For any current or future app UI work, follow the owner's approved [Quiet curiosity design language](docs/Design-Language.md) by default unless the owner explicitly requests a different direction. Keep the Rest-first, calm bedtime flow, semantic colors and shared components, accessible native controls, and honest playback/alarm states. The [approved screen atlas](design/quiet-curiosity/screen-atlas.html) is a visual reference; its future concepts do not imply implemented capabilities.
